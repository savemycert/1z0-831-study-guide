# 1Z0-831 Exam-Day Guide

This page covers the logistics of the Oracle Java SE 25 Developer Professional (1Z0-831) exam: buying and booking it, the computer and room setup, what to have ready, how the exam is built and scored, and what happens afterwards. Every fact below links to the official Oracle page it came from. Oracle updates its policies from time to time, so confirm anything time-sensitive on the linked page before your appointment.

> Also see the [1Z0-831 practice questions](https://www.savemycert.com/practice/oracle-java-se-developer/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=1z0-831-study-guide) and [timed mock exams](https://www.savemycert.com/mocks/oracle-java-se-developer/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=1z0-831-study-guide).

## Buying and booking the exam

Oracle sells exams as exam attempts in your Oracle MyLearn account, bought as an Oracle Exam Learning Subscription. An attempt is applied when you register for an exam, and one attempt is deducted each time you take one. Exam attempts are handled as subscriptions, so registering, canceling and rescheduling all have to happen within the attempt's subscription dates. Oracle warns that it does not guarantee subscriptions bought from anyone other than Oracle or an Oracle authorized reseller.

The 1Z0-831 exam page does not list a price. Check the current price in MyLearn when you buy.

The exam leads to the **Oracle Certified Professional: Java SE 25 Developer** credential. Oracle's recommended training for it is the "Become a Java SE 25 Developer" learning path on MyLearn.

Source: [Java SE 25 Developer Professional (1Z0-831) exam page](https://mylearn.oracle.com/ou/exam/java-se-25-developer-professional-1z0-831/105037/161532/270890), [Oracle Certification Program Guidelines](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/guidelines/)

## Rescheduling and canceling

You must reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment time. Cancellations also need at least 24 hours' notice. If you cancel later than that, you risk forfeiting your exam attempt. Oracle does not cover any costs beyond the exam itself, such as travel, equipment or lost wages.

Source: [Oracle Certification Program Guidelines](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/guidelines/), [Oracle University Exam Preparation](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/exam-prep/)

## Delivery and setup

You register for and schedule 1Z0-831 through its page on Oracle MyLearn. The requirements below come from Oracle's exam-preparation page, which covers exams delivered by Oracle University and does not apply to exams scheduled and delivered by Pearson VUE. Oracle says to check your confirmation email or the exam's registration page if you are unsure which type of exam you are registered for.

**Computer**

- Administrator rights on the machine, and the latest Chrome or Edge on Windows 11, Windows 10 or macOS 13 or later. iOS, Android, ChromeOS, Linux, Windows CE and Windows RT are not supported.
- At least 8 GB of RAM and a single display. Multiple monitors are not allowed. Chromebooks, phones and tablets are not supported.
- A reliable connection of at least 3 Mb/s up and down, with a ping under 100 ms. Do not use a mobile hotspot or a tethered phone, and disconnect from any proxy or VPN.
- A webcam of at least 640x490 at 10 fps, a working microphone and audio, and an English QWERTY keyboard.
- Turn off the browser's incognito and developer modes. Close every other application and tab, leaving only the MyLearn exam page open.
- Run Oracle's exam readiness check before exam day.

**Your space and behaviour**

- You must be the only person in the room, and no other voices may be heard. You must not speak for the whole exam.
- Phones, watches, other electronics, books, notes and other screens are not allowed.
- Keep the camera on and unobstructed, with its shutter open, and keep the microphone on.
- Do not look off-screen, record the screen or take screenshots, or use AI or any other technology for help.
- Pen and paper are not allowed. A digital whiteboard is available in the exam platform.
- Breaking these rules can cost you the attempt, your score, or future attempts.

**Check-in**

Check in at least 30 minutes before your start time, using the Check In button in your confirmation or reminder email, or on your exam page. Candidates who try to check in after the scheduled time may not be admitted.

Source: [Oracle University Exam Preparation](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/exam-prep/), [Java SE 25 Developer Professional (1Z0-831) exam page](https://mylearn.oracle.com/ou/exam/java-se-25-developer-professional-1z0-831/105037/161532/270890)

## What to have ready

You need government-issued photo identification that:

- exactly matches the first and last name you registered with;
- is valid, meaning not expired or invalidated (a clipped corner may indicate an invalidated ID);
- has a recent, recognizable photo.

Accepted forms include a passport, a driver's license, a military ID, a national, state or province ID card, a permanent-resident or alien registration card, a plastic US learner's permit with photo and signature, a school ID, and a European Union card. Expired or significantly damaged IDs are refused, as are IDs from countries subject to US sanctions. Oracle's export restrictions policy has separate rules for nationals of restricted countries, so read it if that applies to you.

Source: [Oracle University Exam Preparation](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/exam-prep/), [Oracle Certification Program Guidelines](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/guidelines/)

## How the exam is structured

| Fact           | 1Z0-831                 |
| -------------- | ----------------------- |
| Format         | Multiple choice         |
| Questions      | 50                      |
| Duration       | 120 minutes             |
| Passing score  | 68%                     |
| Domain weights | Not published by Oracle |

The exam covers ten topic sections, from date, time, text and numeric values through to localization. Oracle publishes the topics but no weight for each section. The percentages in this study guide's notes are estimates based on each section's share of objectives.

Source: [Java SE 25 Developer Professional (1Z0-831) exam page](https://mylearn.oracle.com/ou/exam/java-se-25-developer-professional-1z0-831/105037/161532/270890)

## Scoring and results

Your result appears on screen when the exam ends. The detailed score report is in Oracle CertView. It gives:

- an overall performance summary;
- the final total score;
- pass or fail status;
- a breakdown of your performance across the exam domains.

Oracle does not tell you which questions you missed. Oracle sets each exam version's passing score independently.

After you pass, CertView also has your digital badge and a printable eCertificate. Each badge links to an Oracle-maintained verification page that shows the credential, the date earned, any expiration date and the skills covered.

Oracle's recertification policy sets validity periods for Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Oracle Cloud Applications certifications and for certain Database credentials. Check that policy for the current rules on your credential.

Source: [Oracle University Exam Preparation](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/exam-prep/), [Oracle Certification Program Guidelines](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/guidelines/)

## If you don't pass

You can schedule a retake of a failed exam. Each retake uses another purchased exam attempt. Oracle's retake policy sets an earliest appointment date measured from the failed attempt, but the published wording does not show the length of that wait. Check the current [guidelines](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/guidelines/) or ask Oracle certification support before you book.

You cannot retake an exam you have passed. Creating a new ID to get around the retake policy invalidates the retake's results, with no refund.

The score report's domain breakdown shows which sections to study before your next attempt.

Source: [Oracle Certification Program Guidelines](https://www.oracle.com/education/certification/guidelines/)

## On exam day: practical tips

- **Pace yourself.** 120 minutes for 50 questions is about 2.4 minutes per question. Most items are code to trace, so do not let one long trace eat the time of several short ones.
- **Check compilation first.** "Compilation fails" and runtime exceptions are real answer options. Before working out any output, check every declaration, label and call against the rules.
- **Track types as well as values.** Promotion, overload choice, switch result types and collector value types all depend on the static type at each step.
- **Use the whiteboard.** Pen and paper are not allowed, so sketch references, loop variables and thread order on the digital whiteboard.
